## MongoDB Aggregation Framework

MongoDB Aggregation is used to process, analyze, and calculate data through a pipeline.

* **`$match`** → Filters documents.
* **`$group`** → Groups data and performs calculations like `$sum`.
* **`$project`** → Selects, removes, or formats fields.
* **`$sort`** → Sorts results in ascending (`1`) or descending (`-1`) order.
* **`$lookup`** → Joins data from another collection.
* **`$unwind`** → Converts an array into individual documents.
